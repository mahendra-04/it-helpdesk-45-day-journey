# Day 27 - Ticketing Systems and ITIL

**Status:** Lesson Completed

## Learning Objectives

- Understand how ticketing systems organize support work.
- Learn incident lifecycle, priority, category, SLA, and escalation concepts.
- Write professional ticket updates and closure notes.

## Concepts Learned

- Incident, request, problem, change, priority, impact, urgency, SLA, and escalation.
- Ticket categories, assignment groups, status updates, and user communication.
- ITIL-aligned service management concepts.
- Importance of accurate closure codes and resolution notes.

## Real-World Help Desk Examples

- A password reset ticket is low complexity but high urgency for the user.
- A network outage has high impact and needs escalation.
- A recurring printer issue may become a problem-management candidate.

## Troubleshooting Workflows

1. Capture user, affected service, impact, urgency, and symptoms.
2. Categorize and prioritize the ticket accurately.
3. Troubleshoot and communicate status updates.
4. Escalate with evidence when needed.
5. Close with resolution, user confirmation, and final notes.

## Documentation Examples

- Use clear summary lines and consistent categories.
- Separate user-reported symptoms from technician findings.
- Include escalation reason and evidence.

## Ticket Note Examples

**Issue:** User unable to access shared drive needed for payroll work.

**Technician notes:** Confirmed business impact, checked network connection, verified group membership, and tested path access.

**Resolution:** Added approved access group and confirmed user could open required folder. Ticket closed with user confirmation.

## Key Takeaways

- Ticket quality affects response speed and reporting.
- Impact and urgency drive priority.
- Closure notes should be clear enough for future review.
